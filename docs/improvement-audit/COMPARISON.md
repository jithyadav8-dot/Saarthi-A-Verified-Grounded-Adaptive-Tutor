They are conditional engineering judgments, not competition scores or a feature count. ## What is demonstrably stronger?

Pramana's source has more complete course-to-assessment-to-remediation integration, native/visual multimodal processing and replayable evaluation infrastructure. Saarthi's existing graph has richer prerequisite evidence metadata, and its upload boundary has safeguards absent from the inspected Pramana path. Both already use hybrid retrieval, citation locators, question verification concepts and BKT. Neither has established semantic correctness, calibrated learner mastery or real learning gains merely by implementing those components.

The executed evidence establishes improvements to **Saarthi versus itself**: structured extraction, adversarial contract rejection, prerequisite gating and exactly-once-per-attempt learner updates. It does not establish that the resulting system generally outperforms Pramana.

## Decision rules

Latency/memory/cost, educational benefit, effort/risk and validation gates for each proposed action are in [the scored roadmap](IMPROVEMENT_ROADMAP.md). Exact implementation and compatibility details are in [implementation report](IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md). Native slide extraction was selected because a local fixture proved information loss without needing vision calls. Retrieval was preserved because no controlled evidence favored a replacement. Video lessons and uncalibrated learner complexity were deferred because they lack both the required evaluated content foundation and evidence of benefit.
